## Artisan Story & Craftsmanship / 工艺与文化叙事

* **EN:**  
  Drawing inspiration from maritime Silk Road treasures reclaimed from deep-sea shipwrecks, this tasting cup embodies the quiet beauty of aged porcelain. Sculpted from native Jingdezhen kaolin clay and thrown on the wheel with a gentle flared rim, the vessel serves as a canvas for classical underglaze cobalt art. 
  
  The artisan meticulously renders a weathered, ancient pine tree (*Gushu*) using layered mineral cobalt washes—each needle and twisted bough capturing the enduring spirit of mountain mist and quietude. Along the rim, a deliberate and poetic accent of traditional *kintsugi* (crafted with natural raw lacquer and finished with genuine gold powder) honors the philosophy of finding perfection in impermanence. The resulting tactile finish is neither sterile nor glossy, but softly aged, offering a silky, jade-like patina that deepens with every tea ritual.

* **ZH:**  
  本品灵感撷取自“海上丝绸之路”沉船遗珍的海捞古韵，流露着经年岁序沉淀出的宁静致远。甄选景德镇原矿高白瓷泥手工拉坯，杯身线条自足部微收后向外优雅微撇，器型端庄适手。
  
  画师以天然矿物钴料为墨，在素坯上以干湿浓淡的手法层层晕染出一株苍劲古松。笔触细腻生动，松针苍茫若隐于山野薄雾之中，寄托高洁长青的风骨。杯沿处施以传统天然生漆与纯金粉精工修补的金缮工艺，不掩瑕疵，反成点睛之笔，生动诠释东方“物哀”与“惜物”的哲学意涵。釉面褪去浮光，呈现出海捞瓷特有的温润如玉之质，越经茶汤摩挲，越显醇厚光泽。
