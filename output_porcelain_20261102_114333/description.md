## Artisan Story & Craftsmanship / 工艺与文化叙事

* **EN:**  
  Handcrafted by legacy potters using mineral-rich kaolin clay, this piece undergoes a grueling 72-hour continuous pine-wood firing exceeding 1,300°C. In the fluctuating heart of the wood kiln, volatile pine ash drifts across the vitrifying surface, fusing organically with the underglaze cobalt (青花). The result is a subtle, wabi-sabi interaction: dynamic cobalt bleeding (*yunsan*), delicate pinhole textures, and natural iron halos that modern gas and electric kilns cannot replicate. Its flared rim (*chang-kou*) is intentionally profiled to release subtle top-notes of fine tea while resting comfortably against the lips.

* **ZH:**  
  甄选景德镇原矿高白泥，由经验丰富的陶人手工拉坯成型，施以天然矿物钴料绘就折枝花卉。入传统龙窑/倒焰柴窑，经长达数十小时的松木高温烈火（1300°C以上）历练。松木油脂与草木飞灰自然沉降，与青花釉料在剧烈还原气氛中交融互化，形成温润似玉的哑光质感与含蓄深邃的“铁锈斑”与“晕散”效果。撇口造型兼顾聚香与贴唇适口性，每件器物皆因柴窑窑位与火痕不同而独一无二。
