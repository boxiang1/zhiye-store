## Artisan Story & Craftsmanship / 工艺与文化叙事

**EN:**  
Conceived in Jingdezhen by ZHIYE’s master potters, the "Submerged Pine" tasting cup pays homage to the fabled porcelain cargos that rested silently on ancient maritime silk roads. Thrown from refined native kaolin clay, the body reveals an atmospheric landscape where ancient tree needles and gnarls emerge through delicate cobalt diffusion—capturing the serene stillness of deep mountain mist and underwater light.

Marked by a deliberate gold *Kintsugi* (金缮) flourish on its flared lip, this vessel transforms historical imperfection into its most striking feature. Applying ancestral urushi lacquer dusted with pure gold leaf, the artisan embraces the wabi-sabi philosophy: wounds are not hidden, but illuminated. The softly muted glaze, reminiscent of ocean-smoothed ceramic relics, rests weightlessly in the hand, guiding the tea liquor with exceptional sensory clarity.

**ZH:**  
由“执冶”景德镇匠人亲手拉坯成型，本品以海上丝绸之路沉船瓷器为灵感，探寻岁月沉淀的造物诗学。胎体精选老矿高岭土，青花以天然矿物钴料挥毫泼墨，古树虬枝与松针随窑火温差自然晕散，意境幽邃如隐山岚雾，又似深海遗光。

杯沿一道精微的天然大漆24K金缮，点化残缺为独一无二的精神徽章。金缮非为遮掩，而为赋新生——它将文人器物的克制、海捞器皿的风化古朴与日本侘寂美学融会贯通。微温的类玉抚感，辅以舒展适口的微撇口设计，让每一次茶汤流淌都化作一场穿越时空的心灵对话。

## Key Features & Specifications / 核心规格与细节

* **Clay Body / 胎质骨料:** Hand-thrown Jingdezhen high-purity kaolin clay; dense, fine-grained, and resonant (景德镇手工精拉纯料高岭土，胎骨致密温润).
* **Glaze & Pigment / 釉色与青料:** Hand-painted mineral cobalt oxide under a softened, semi-matte antique jade-white glaze (手绘天然矿物钴料，仿古海捞水洗质感微润白釉).
* **Restoration Craft / 修复工艺:** Authentic hand-applied Japanese Urushi tree lacquer dusted with genuine 24K gold powder (传统纯天然生漆打底，嵌饰24K足金真金粉金缮).
* **Silhouette & Rim / 器型与口沿:** Ergonomic flared tasting rim (*Piekou*), engineered to distribute tea aroma and temperature smoothly across the palate (经典撇口杯型，顺应唇形，出汤聚香灵动).
* **Dimensions & Capacity / 规格参数:** 
  * Diameter: ~7.4 cm (2.9 in)
  * Height: ~5.0 cm (2.0 in)
  * Functional Capacity: ~65 ml (2.2 fl oz)
